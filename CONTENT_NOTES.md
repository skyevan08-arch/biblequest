# Starter Lesson References

1. David & Goliath
   - 1 Samuel 17
   - CCC 1814–1816: faith

2. Noah's Ark
   - Genesis 6–9
   - CCC 56–58: covenant with Noah
   - CCC 1094: Old Testament typology and Baptism

3. The Good Samaritan
   - Luke 10:25–37
   - CCC 1822–1825: charity and love of neighbor

4. Moses & the Red Sea
   - Exodus 14
   - CCC 1094: Red Sea as a figure of Baptism
   - CCC 2574–2577: Moses and intercessory prayer

5. The Birth of Jesus
   - Luke 2:1–20
   - CCC 525–526: the Nativity, poverty, and humility

6. Jesus Calms the Storm
   - Mark 4:35–41
   - CCC 547–550: Jesus' signs and miracles

The website text is a learning paraphrase and does not replace reading Scripture itself.


7. The Prodigal Son
   - Scripture: Luke 15:11–32
   - Catholic connection: CCC 1439 (conversion, repentance, and the merciful father)

8. Feeding the Five Thousand
   - Scripture: John 6:1–15
   - Catholic connection: CCC 1335 (multiplication of loaves as a figure of Eucharistic abundance)
